A story brand, built to last.

Sandstone started with animated stories, but it is a brand. Every idea begins as a grain of sand and is shaped into something solid: films, products and a community that grows with each release.

We tell stories on YouTube and Instagram, make things you can own, and show how it is all made.

Questions, answered

The short answers to what people ask us most.

What is Sandstone?

Sandstone is a story brand. We make animated stories and build a brand around them: films, products and a community.

Where can I watch the films?

On YouTube and Instagram. The Films page on this site plays them too.

When does the shop open?

The first drop is coming soon. Follow us on Instagram to see it first.

Can we work together?

Yes. Email us from the Contact page with your idea.

Can I share or reuse your stories?

Sharing our links is always welcome. For any other use, please email us first.
